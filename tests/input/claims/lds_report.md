## Question

What is Loeys-Dietz syndrome, and how does it differ from Marfan syndrome?

## Output

# Loeys-Dietz syndrome

## Genetics

Loeys-Dietz syndrome is caused by heterozygous variants in genes of the TGF-beta
signalling pathway, most often TGFBR1 or TGFBR2.[1] Loeys et al. described it
in 2005 in families with arterial tortuosity and aortic aneurysms.[1] Variants in
SMAD3 and TGFB2 were later found to cause the same phenotype. [3]

## Vascular disease

Aortic aneurysms in Loeys-Dietz syndrome dissect at smaller diameters than in
Marfan syndrome [2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16928994/), so surgery is
recommended earlier. Arterial tortuosity, e.g. of the head and neck vessels, is
a hallmark of the disorder.[3]

## Other features

- Hypertelorism and bifid uvula or cleft palate are common.[1]
- Unlike in Marfan syndrome, ectopia lentis is not a feature.[2]
