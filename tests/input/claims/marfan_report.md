## Question

What causes Marfan syndrome, and how is it managed?

## Output

# Marfan syndrome

## Genetics

Marfan syndrome is an autosomal dominant disorder caused by pathogenic variants in **FBN1** [1]. It is not caused by variants in *TGFBR2*, which instead cause Loeys-Dietz syndrome [2].

## Clinical features

| Feature | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Aortic root dilation | 60-80% of adults |
| Ectopia lentis | about 60% |

Aortic dissection is the leading cause of death (see [the 2010 Ghent criteria](https://doi.org/10.1136/jmg.2009.072785)).

## Management

Beta-blockers may slow aortic root growth [3].
