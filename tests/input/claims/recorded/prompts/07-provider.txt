Aortic aneurysms in Loeys-Dietz syndrome dissect at smaller diameters than in
Marfan syndrome [2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16928994/), so surgery is
recommended earlier. Arterial tortuosity, e.g. of the head and neck vessels, is
a hallmark of the disorder.[3]
